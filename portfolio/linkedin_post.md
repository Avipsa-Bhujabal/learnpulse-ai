# LinkedIn launch draft

I built LearnPulse, a research-only OULAD project asking whether changes from a learner's own previous activity help predict future recorded VLE inactivity.

The work spans 10.7M activity records, DuckDB/Parquet data engineering, leakage-safe personal baselines, unseen-course evaluation, nested calibration, explainability, subgroup auditing, temporal warning stability and retrospective review-capacity simulation. I also packaged a separate reference artifact behind FastAPI with strict validation, atomic batch scoring and drift monitoring.

The most important lesson was methodological: useful ranking signal does not establish causation, psychological disengagement, calibrated individual risk or intervention benefit. Course variation, rare outcomes and false-alert costs remain central limitations.

The repository is designed as a reproducible ML engineering and research portfolio—not a deployed student decision system.
